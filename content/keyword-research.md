# Bhumitra keyword research (29 Oct 2026)

Method: Google India autocomplete (`hl=en`, `gl=in`, plus `hl=or` for Odia) for ~140 seed
queries about Odisha land. Autocomplete is ordered by search popularity, so a phrase that
shows up across many seeds is in high demand. Facts in the guides were checked against the
official portals the same day: Bhulekh (bhulekh.ori.nic.in), BhuNaksha Odisha
(bhunakshaodisha.nic.in), IGR Odisha (igrodisha.gov.in: fee table, document list, online
services) and e-Pauti (odishalandrevenue.nic.in).

Limits: autocomplete gives relative demand, not monthly volumes. For volumes, run these
through Google Keyword Planner or Search Console once the site is live.

## Odia-language searches

Odia autocomplete is thin, so English and transliterated queries carry most of the demand:
ଜମି ପଟ୍ଟା, ଜମି ମାପ, ଜମି କିସମ, ଭୂଲେଖ ଓଡିଶା, and romanised "jami pata odisha",
"odisha jami map", "jami mapa calculator", "odisha jami naksha". Worth targeting with Odia
versions of the top guides later.
