The Record of Rights shows who holds a plot today. It does not show everything that has happened to it. An Encumbrance Certificate (EC) fills that gap. It lists the registered transactions on a property over a period you choose: sales, gifts, mortgages, leases and more.

Why the EC matters

  • It shows whether the land has been sold before, possibly to someone else.
  • It shows registered mortgages that may still be outstanding.
  • It's in IGR Odisha's list of documents for registration: an EC from the date the RoR was published by settlement or consolidation up to the date the deed is executed.
  • Banks ask for it before giving a home or land loan.

How to apply for an EC online in Odisha

  1. Go to igrodisha.gov.in and log in or register as a new user.
  2. Choose Online Encumbrance Certificate (also listed under Property as "Apply for Encumbrance Certificate").
  3. Enter the property details exactly as in the RoR: district, registration office, village, khata and plot numbers.
  4. Choose the period to be searched.
  5. Pay the fee online and note the application number. You can follow it with Track Application Status.

You can also apply in person at the Sub-Registrar office that covers the village.

How to read an EC

Each entry usually shows the deed number and date, the type of deed, the parties, and the property described. Read it with these questions in mind:

  • Does the chain make sense? Each sale should lead to the next seller. A gap or an unknown buyer needs explaining.
  • Is there a mortgage? If yes, ask for the bank's release (reconveyance) deed.
  • Any agreement for sale or power of attorney? These can give someone else rights in the land.
  • Does the property description match? Plot numbers and areas should match the RoR.

What an EC does not show

An EC covers only registered documents. It won't show unregistered agreements, family arrangements or pending court cases. Read the RoR's remarks column too, and ask a local advocate to check for litigation before a large purchase.

Check the plot first, then order the EC

An EC application needs correct khata and plot numbers. Get them right first: tap the plot in Bhumitra to open its RoR, and note the numbers. Bhumitra's land report also shows registered sales for the plot where they are available online, which is a quick preview before you order the full certificate.
