When you register land in Odisha, the stamp duty isn't based only on the price you agree. The state sets a minimum value for every area, called the benchmark valuation. Knowing it helps you check a quoted price, budget for registration and spot a deal that's been under-valued on paper.

What benchmark value is

The Inspector General of Registration (IGR), Odisha fixes benchmark values for land across the state. Each rate depends on:

  • the registration office and village (mouza) or urban ward,
  • the kisam (land type), such as gharabari or agricultural,
  • sometimes the location within the village, such as land along a main road.

It's published as a rate per acre, per decimal or per square foot, and revised from time to time.

How it decides your stamp duty

For a standard sale of land, the duty is 5% and the registration fee 2%. On ₹12,00,000 that's ₹60,000 + ₹24,000 = ₹84,000. See our stamp duty guide for more examples.

How to check benchmark value on IGR Odisha

  1. Open igrodisha.gov.in.
  2. Find BenchMark Valuation (on the home page and under Property).
  3. Select the district and registration office.
  4. Select the village or area, then the kisam.
  5. Read the rate and its unit. Multiply by the plot area to get the benchmark value.

IGR Odisha also has a Registration & Stamp Duty Calculator that works out the charges once you know the value.

The quicker way: open the plot

Choosing the right office, village and kisam is where people go wrong. In Bhumitra, tap the plot on the map and the land report shows the government benchmark rate for that plot, where one is published, with the registration office it comes from. It uses the plot's area and kisam from the RoR, then estimates the stamp duty and registration fee.

Bhumitra won't guess a rate. If more than one registration office serves a village, it asks you to pick one. If no rate is published for the plot, it says so rather than borrowing a number from somewhere else.

Working with units

Rates may be quoted per acre or per decimal while you think in square feet. Remember 1 acre = 100 decimals and 1 decimal = 435.6 sq ft. So a rate of ₹5,00,000 per decimal is about ₹1,148 per sq ft. Our land units guide has the full conversion table.
