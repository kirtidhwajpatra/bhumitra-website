Help centre

How can we help?

For the fastest help, use the support option in Bhumitra. You can also email our support team directly.

Email Bhumitra supportsupport@getbhumitra.in Open Bhumitra on the App StoreDownload or update the iPhone app

Before you write

Please include the app version, your iOS version, a short description of what happened and a screenshot if you can. Never send passwords, Apple ID credentials or sensitive identity documents by email.

Common questions

A land record or map looks wrong

Land information is shown from public sources and may change or be incomplete. Verify it against the official Record of Rights and the concerned Tahasil or revenue office before acting on it.

My location is not found

Allow Bhumitra to use your location while using the app, then try again outdoors with a stable connection. You can also choose a village manually.

I need help with a purchase or credits

Use the in-app Help & Support screen so the message includes helpful diagnostics. For subscriptions, Apple manages billing and renewals in your Apple account settings.

How do I delete my account?

Open your account in Bhumitra and select the account-deletion option. If you need help, email support@getbhumitra.in.
