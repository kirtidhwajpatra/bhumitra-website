Buying land

Stamp duty and registration charges in Odisha, with examples

How much does it cost to register land in Odisha? Stamp duty of 5% (4% for women buyers), a 2% registration fee, and how the government benchmark value decides what you pay.

By the Bhumitra team · 29 October 2026 · 2 min read

When you buy land in Odisha, the price you agree with the seller is not the whole cost. The state charges stamp duty and a registration fee when the sale deed is registered. Knowing these in advance helps you budget and spot a deal that has been under-valued on paper.

The rates

ChargeRate
Stamp duty (standard)5%
Stamp duty (woman buyer)4%
Registration fee2%

These are the standard rates for a sale deed of immovable property used in Bhumitra's estimates. Different deed types (gift, partition, lease, settlement among family) have different rates, and the state revises rates from time to time. The registration office confirms the final figure.

What value is the duty charged on?

The Inspector General of Registration (IGR), Odisha fixes a benchmark value for land in each area. It depends on the village or ward, the registration office and the kisam (land type), and is published as a rate per acre, per decimal or per sq ft.

Duty is normally charged on the higher of:

  • the price written in the sale deed, and
  • the benchmark value of the land being sold.

So writing a lower price on the deed does not reduce the duty below the benchmark. It does create legal and tax risk for both sides.

Worked example

You're buying 10 decimals of land for ₹10,00,000, and the benchmark value for the plot works out lower than that.

Standard buyerWoman buyer
Stamp duty₹50,000 (5%)₹40,000 (4%)
Registration fee₹20,000 (2%)₹20,000 (2%)
Total₹70,000₹60,000

If instead the benchmark value were ₹12,00,000, the duty and fee would be worked out on ₹12,00,000: ₹84,000 for a standard buyer.

Other costs to budget for

  • Charges for the Encumbrance Certificate and certified copies.
  • Mutation at the Tahasil after registration, so the RoR shows your name.
  • Conversion fees, if agricultural land will be used for building.
  • Professional fees for a lawyer or deed writer.

Estimating this in Bhumitra

Open a plot in Bhumitra and the land report shows the government benchmark rate for it, where one is published, along with the registration office it comes from. Enter the deal price and the app estimates stamp duty and registration fee, using the plot's area and land type from the record.

The app will not guess. If more than one registration office serves the village, it asks you to pick one rather than show a rate that may belong to a different office. If no benchmark is published for the plot, it says so and works from your price. The benchmark is an official figure for registration, not a market valuation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the stamp duty for land in Odisha?

For a standard sale of immovable property, stamp duty in Odisha is 5% of the value. Women buyers pay a concessional 4%. Rates are set by the state and can change, so confirm the current rate with IGR Odisha or the registration office.

What is the registration fee in Odisha?

The registration fee is 2% of the value, paid in addition to stamp duty.

What is the benchmark value of land?

The benchmark value (government guideline value) is the minimum value per unit area that the Inspector General of Registration fixes for each location and land type. Duty is normally charged on the higher of the deal price and the benchmark value.
