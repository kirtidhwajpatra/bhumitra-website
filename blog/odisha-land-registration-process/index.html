Land registration in Odisha: process, slot booking, documents and charges

A step-by-step guide to registering a land sale in Odisha: online deed submission and slot booking on IGR Odisha, the official list of documents, stamp duty, registration fee and other charges.

Registering a sale deed is the step that makes a land purchase official. In Odisha it's handled by the Inspector General of Registration (IGR) through Sub-Registrar and District Sub-Registrar offices. Much of the paperwork can now be done online at igrodisha.gov.in, so you spend less time at the office.

The registration process at a glance

Step 1: check the land before anything else

Confirm the seller's name, shares, kisam and remarks in the Record of Rights, see the plot on the map, and get an Encumbrance Certificate. You can do the first checks in a few minutes by tapping the plot in Bhumitra. Our 10-point checklist lists everything to verify.

Step 2: prepare the deed

IGR Odisha's e-Registration lets you prepare the deed online using a common format and change the recitals as needed. Many buyers still have an advocate or licensed deed writer draft it, which is sensible for anything unusual such as inherited land or several co-owners.

Step 3: book a slot online

  1. Register or log in on igrodisha.gov.in.
  2. Choose Online Slot Booking for Deed and submit the property and party details.
  3. Pick the registration office and an available date and time.
  4. Use View & Print Slot Booking to keep a copy.

If a party can't travel, IGR also lists a Visit Deed Request service for registration at a residence in specific cases.

Step 4: documents to carry

From IGR Odisha's published list of documents for registration:

  • True copy of the document to be registered.
  • All documents relating to ownership of the property.
  • Valid photo ID of all executants, claimants and the identifier (EPIC, Aadhaar, driving licence, passport or PAN).
  • Three passport-size colour photographs of each executant and claimant.
  • PAN of the executants and claimants if the document is valued over ₹10 lakh, or Form 60 if there's no PAN.
  • Encumbrance Certificate from the date the RoR was published by settlement or consolidation up to the date of execution.
  • Declaration in Form A if no building exists on the land, or Form B if a house or structure exists.
  • Registered power of attorney and the attorney holder's ID, if an attorney is signing.
  • Permission from the Revenue Officer when land is transferred by an SC/ST person to a non-SC/ST person.
  • NOC from the Endowments authority for property belonging to a deity.

Step 5: registration charges

For a sale of immovable property, IGR Odisha's fee table lists:

ChargeAmount
Stamp duty5% of the value
Registration fee2% of the value
Demarcation fee₹20 per plot
RoR postal delivery fee₹40
RoR user fee₹80 per khata
Deed postal fee₹60 up to 25 pages, ₹80 above

The value used is normally the higher of the deal price and the benchmark value. Other deeds have different rates. For example, IGR lists gift of immovable property at 5% + 2%, partition at 2% + 2% and settlement at 2% + 2%. See our stamp duty guide for worked examples.

Fees from IGR Odisha's published table, checked October 2026. Rates change; confirm on igrodisha.gov.in or at the office before paying.

Step 6: at the registration office

Both parties, or their attorney holders, appear with the identifier at the booked time. The office checks IDs, takes photographs and thumb impressions, and registers the deed. You can collect the registered deed or have it posted.

Step 7: don't forget mutation

Registration transfers ownership, but the Record of Rights changes only after mutation. Follow up until your name appears in the RoR.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a slot for land registration in Odisha?

Use Online Slot Booking for Deed on igrodisha.gov.in. Register, submit the deed details online, choose the registration office and an available time, and pay the stamp duty and fees online.

What documents are needed for land registration in Odisha?

IGR Odisha's list includes a true copy of the deed, ownership documents for the property, photo ID and three passport-size photos of every executant and claimant, PAN or Form 60 if the value is over Rs 10 lakh, an Encumbrance Certificate, and the Form A or Form B declaration about structures on the land.

What are land registration charges in Odisha?

For a sale of immovable property, IGR Odisha lists stamp duty of 5% and a registration fee of 2%, plus small fixed fees such as a per-plot demarcation fee, an RoR user fee per khata and postal charges.

Do buyer and seller both need to go to the registration office?

Yes, the executant (seller) and claimant (buyer), or their registered attorney holders, must appear with ID, along with an identifier.
