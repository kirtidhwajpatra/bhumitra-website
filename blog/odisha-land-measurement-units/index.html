Odisha land measurement units: decimal, acre, guntha, bigha and sq ft

1 acre = 100 decimal = 43,560 sq ft. A clear conversion table for Odisha land units, including decimal to sq ft, guntha, mana, bigha, katha, cent and hectare, with worked examples.

Odisha's land records measure area in acres and decimals. Buyers, builders and brokers, though, talk in square feet, gunthas, bighas or cents. Mixing these up is one of the easiest ways to overpay for land. This page gives the conversions and shows how to check them.

The two units in your RoR

The Record of Rights uses:

  • 1 acre = 100 decimals
  • 1 decimal = 435.6 sq ft (about 40.47 m² or 48.4 sq yd)

So an area written as 0.150 acres is 15 decimals, which is 6,534 sq ft.

Bigha, katha and guntha are not standard across India. The values above are the ones Bhumitra uses for Odisha: 1 bigha is a third of an acre and 1 katha is 1/20 of a bigha. Local usage can differ between areas, so confirm the value with the seller in writing.

Worked examples

Decimal to sq ft

A plot of 5 decimals is 5 × 435.6 = 2,178 sq ft. That's roughly a 45 ft × 48 ft house plot.

Sq ft to decimal

A broker offers 2,400 sq ft. Divide by 435.6: 2,400 ÷ 435.6 = 5.51 decimals.

Price per decimal to price per sq ft

Land is often quoted per decimal. At ₹3,00,000 per decimal, the price per sq ft is 3,00,000 ÷ 435.6 ≈ ₹689 per sq ft.

Check the area against the record

The area that counts legally is the one in the RoR, not the one in a brochure. Before you agree a price:

  1. Open the plot's record and note the area in acres or decimals.
  2. Convert it to the unit the seller is quoting.
  3. If the plot is part of a larger holding with co-owners, work out the share being sold.

Frequently asked questions

How many sq ft is 1 decimal in Odisha?

1 decimal is 435.6 sq ft, or about 40.47 square metres. 100 decimals make 1 acre (43,560 sq ft).

How many decimals make one acre?

100 decimals make one acre in Odisha land records.

Is a cent the same as a decimal?

Yes. A cent is one hundredth of an acre, which is the same as a decimal: 435.6 sq ft.

How big is a guntha in Odisha?

The guntha varies by state. Bhumitra's Odisha converter uses 1 guntha = 4 decimals = 1,742.4 sq ft. In Maharashtra and Karnataka a guntha is 1/40 acre (1,089 sq ft), so always check which value a document assumes.
