Land mutation in Odisha: how to apply and check mutation status

What mutation means in Odisha, when you need it after a sale or inheritance, how to apply at the Tahasil or online, the documents needed, and how to check mutation case status.

You bought the land, the deed is registered, and the money is paid. But when you open the Record of Rights, it still shows the seller's name. That's normal until mutation is done. Mutation is the step that updates the government's land record to show the new owner.

What mutation means

Mutation (in Odia, often called namantaran) is a case decided by the Tahasildar. It changes the owner details in the RoR after:

  • a sale or gift registered at the registration office,
  • inheritance after an owner's death,
  • a partition among co-owners,
  • a court decree or a government grant (patta).

Registration proves the transfer. Mutation updates the revenue record. You need both.

The mutation process

How to apply

After a sale

In Odisha, details of registered sale deeds are shared with the Tahasil so a mutation case can be started from the registration itself. Don't assume it has happened. Ask the registration office for the mutation case number, or apply to the Tahasildar if no case has started. Where online revenue services are available in your district, you can apply and pay through the state's portal instead of visiting.

After inheritance

The heirs apply to the Tahasildar for mutation by succession. Every legal heir should be included with their share. Leaving someone out leads to disputes later, especially when the land is sold.

Documents usually needed

CaseDocuments
Sale or giftRegistered deed copy, current RoR, buyer's ID, land revenue receipt
InheritanceDeath certificate, legal heir or family member certificate, current RoR, IDs of heirs
PartitionRegistered partition deed or court decree, RoR, IDs of all co-owners

Requirements vary by Tahasil and change over time. Ask the Tahasil or check the portal before applying.

How to check mutation status

  1. Keep the mutation case number from your application or acknowledgement.
  2. Check the case status on the state's land record or revenue services portal, or ask at the Tahasil counter.
  3. When the case is allowed, open the RoR for the khata on Bhulekh, or tap the plot in Bhumitra, and confirm your name, share and plot list.

If your name doesn't appear weeks after the order, ask the Tahasil whether the record correction has been posted.

Common reasons mutation gets stuck

  • Objections from a co-owner or neighbour during the notice period.
  • Mismatch between the deed and the RoR, such as a wrong plot number or area.
  • Missing heirs in an inheritance case.
  • Unpaid land revenue. Clear the khajana first; see how to pay it online.

Why it matters when you buy land

Before buying, check that the seller's name is actually in the RoR. If the seller inherited the land and never mutated it, insist that the mutation is completed before registration. Otherwise you are buying from someone the record doesn't yet recognise. Our land buying checklist covers this and nine other checks.

Frequently asked questions

What is mutation of land in Odisha?

Mutation is the update of the Record of Rights to show a new owner after a sale, gift, inheritance, partition or court order. It is done by the Tahasildar through a mutation case.

Is mutation compulsory after buying land?

Registration transfers ownership, but the RoR changes only after mutation. Without it, the record still shows the seller, which causes trouble for loans, land revenue payment and any later sale.

How do I check mutation status in Odisha?

Use the mutation case number from your application or the Tahasil. Check the case status on the state's land record or revenue services portal, or ask at the Tahasil. Once allowed, the new name appears in the RoR on Bhulekh.

How is mutation done after the owner dies?

The legal heirs apply to the Tahasildar for mutation by inheritance, with the death certificate and a legal heir or family member certificate. Once the case is allowed, the RoR is corrected in the heirs' names with their shares.
