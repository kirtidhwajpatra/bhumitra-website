Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026. This policy explains how Bhumitra handles information when you use the app and this website.

Bhumitra is an independent service that helps people explore publicly available Odisha land-record information. We respect your privacy and only use information needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

Information we process

Account information

If you create an account or sign in with Apple or Google, we process the account identifier and the information made available by that sign-in provider. We use it to provide your account, purchases and support.

Location and land-search information

The app asks for location permission only when you choose a feature that needs your current position. We use that location to help identify the relevant area or plot. Search inputs and public land-record results are processed to provide the requested result. Saved lands and recent searches are stored on your device unless a feature clearly tells you otherwise.

Usage and diagnostic information

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature use, diagnose crashes and improve reliability. These services receive a pseudonymous app identifier, device and app-version information, and technical event data. We do not use this information to sell personal data.

Payments

In-app payments are processed by Apple. Bhumitra does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

How we use information

  • Provide land search, mapping, account and purchase features.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot problems.
  • Measure and improve app performance and features.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

When information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help operate Bhumitra, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, crash-reporting and payment providers, or when required by law. Land records and maps shown in Bhumitra come from public government sources; Bhumitra is not affiliated with the Government of Odisha.

Retention and your choices

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including account, payment and legal requirements. You can manage location permission in iOS Settings and delete your account from the Bhumitra account settings. Deleting an account removes the associated account data handled by our service, subject to records we must retain for legal, fraud-prevention or transaction purposes.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No system is completely secure, so please use a current device and protect your sign-in credentials.

Children

Bhumitra is not directed to children under the age at which they can legally consent to use online services in their location. Please do not create an account for a child who is not permitted to use the service.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Bhumitra changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@getbhumitra.in.
