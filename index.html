The problem

Checking a piece of land shouldn't take weeks.

Land is usually a family's biggest asset, and the information about it has always been hard to get.

Rounds of office visits

Tahsil and RI office trips, long queues and lost workdays, only to find out who a plot belongs to.

Numbers nobody remembers

Government portals ask for Khata and plot numbers first. Most people only know where the land is.

Relying on someone's word

Boundaries, ownership and price usually come from a broker, and you can't easily check any of it.

Bhumitra works from where you're standing. Tap once and the official record for that plot opens on your phone.

How it works

From field to official record in 3 taps.

You don't need forms or numbers. If you can open a map, you can use Bhumitra.

1

Tap "locate"

Bhumitra finds your district, tahsil and village from your GPS on its own. You can also pick a village by hand.

2

Tap your plot

Official cadastral boundaries are drawn over satellite imagery. You can see exactly where your land starts and ends.

3

See the official record

You get the owner, Khata, area and land type from the Record of Rights, plus the government value and the cost to register.

What took weeks of paperwork now takes under a minute.
