Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Visit Help & support.

Where does Bhumitra get its land records?

Records of Rights come from the Government of Odisha's public Bhulekh portal (bhulekh.ori.nic.in). Plot boundaries come from the state's cadastral maps, and land values from Odisha IGR. Bhumitra is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Government of Odisha.

Do I need a khata or plot number?

No. Choose a village or tap locate, and the plot boundaries appear on a satellite map. Tap the plot to open its record. If you do have a number, you can also search by plot number, khata number or plot unique ID.

Is Bhumitra free?

The app is free to download and your first searches are free. After that you can buy a one-time search pack, whose credits never expire, or subscribe to Monthly Unlimited. A plot you've already opened stays open with no second charge.

Can I use a Bhumitra record for legal purposes?

No. Bhumitra shows records for information only. For a sale, loan or court matter, get a certified copy of the Record of Rights from your Tahasil office.

Which districts are covered?

All 30 districts of Odisha. Plot maps are available wherever the state has published cadastral map data for the village.

Is Bhumitra available on Android?

Bhumitra is on the App Store for iPhone now. The Android app is coming soon to Google Play.
